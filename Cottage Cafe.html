A little cottage, brewed with love

Cottage Cafe began as a family kitchen with one espresso machine and a lot of stubborn hope. Today it’s still a family place: beans are roasted in small batches, dough rests overnight and every dessert is baked before sunrise.

We believe good coffee deserves a comfortable chair, and good food deserves unhurried company. Come for a quick cappuccino or stay the whole afternoon with a book.
